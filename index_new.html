About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in theoretical and computational neuroscience in Trinity College Dublin and Oxford University with Dr Seán Froudist-Walsh.

I completed my PhD under the supervision of Dr Cian O'Donnell, in the Intelligent Systems Research Centre, Ulster University, and was visiting PhD Researcher at the University of Bristol, under Dr Conor Houghton (School of Engineering Mathematics and Technology).
I studied Mathematics (MA Dubl) as a Naughton Scholar at Trinity College Dublin, and obtained a PGCE (Mathematics) and MRes from Queen's University Belfast.

I am an active member of the Institute of Mathematics & its Applications (IMA) as Treasurer of the Irish branch, and a member of the Society for Industrial & Applied Mathematics (SIAM), and Neuroscience Ireland (NSI).

Academic Material

See here for more detail, but I am mainly interested in biological and artificial recurrent neural networks – their structure, dynamics, and computation.
I approach these questions using tools from complex systems theory, dynamical systems theory, topology, and machine learning, seeking to understand how the structure and dynamics of cortical networks give rise to complex cognitive function.
I am also interested in mathematical models and analysis of synaptic, neuronal, and network dynamics, particularly in the context of development, maturation, and computation.
